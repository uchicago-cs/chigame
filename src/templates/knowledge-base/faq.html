Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Knowledge Base?

The Knowledge Base is a collection of user-contributed guides and resources for games, curated and moderated to ensure quality and accuracy.

How do I find guides?

Use the search bar on the landing page to look for guides by game name or keywords. You can also browse guides by category using the filter options. If a category option displays "No guides found," there exists some gam under that category, but there is no published guide for the game yet.

How do I contribute a guide?

Click on "Contributor View" in the header. From there, you can upload a new guide or manage your existing submissions. Guides must be in Markdown format.

What happens after I submit a guide?

Submitted guides are reviewed by moderators. You will be notified if your guide is accepted, rejected, or if changes are requested via the "Manage My Guides" page.

How do I edit or update my guide?

Go to "Contributor View" and select "Manage My Guides." You can re-upload or update your guides from there if moderators have requested changes.

What are the guidelines for contributors?

Please ensure your guides are accurate, well-formatted, and helpful.

How does moderation work?

Moderators review all new and updated guides for quality and compliance. You may receive feedback or requests for changes before your guide is published.
